# MUGI Ramen & Izakaya — Menu Photo Matching

Matches every item in `menus/phan-chu-trinh.md`, `menus/golden-palace.md`, and
`menus/mon-city.md` against the 144 files in `menu_pictures/`. Matching is
based on the Vietnamese dish name embedded in the photo filename (diacritics/
case-insensitive), cross-checked against the Japanese name/price where the
filename includes one. Because Phan Chu Trinh and Golden Palace share almost
the entire menu, most photos are listed for both. Where a filename explicitly
says "PHAN CHU TRINH" / "PCT" or "MỸ ĐÌNH", that photo is only used for that
specific branch's row.

Legend: ✅ = matched photo. **MISSING PHOTO** = no file in `menu_pictures/`
corresponds to this item.
