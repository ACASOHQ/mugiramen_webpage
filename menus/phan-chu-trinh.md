# MUGI Ramen & Izakaya — Menu (Phan Chu Trinh branch)

Transcribed from `assets/Menu cơ sở Phan Chu Trinh.pdf` (26 pages). Back
cover (p.24) confirms this is "CS2": **Số 23 Phan Chu Trinh, Cửa Nam, Hà
Nội** — matches the site's `phan-chu-trinh` location. Prices are printed
VAT-exclusive.

> **Mostly identical to Golden Palace, with two real exceptions:** the
> Nước hoa quả (fresh juice), Nước Dasani, and Trà (tea) prices are higher
> here than at Golden Palace/Mỹ Đình, and the **Sake list is substantially
> different** — 2 sakes exclusive to this branch, 2 Golden Palace sakes not
> offered here, and 2 that overlap but with different pricing/naming.
> Ramen, Sashimi, Sushi, Sushi Rolls, Salad, Otsumami, Agemono, Yakimono,
> Cơm, Udon, soft drinks, Cocktail, Bia, Soju, and Corkage fees are all
> byte-identical to `golden-palace.md` — confirmed page by page, not
> assumed.

Format: **Vietnamese name** *(English name)* — Japanese name — price
