# MUGI Ramen & Izakaya — Menu (Mỹ Đình / Mon City, Hàm Nghi branch)

Transcribed from `assets/Menu Mỹ Đình .pdf` (26 pages). This is the "CS1"
branch per the PDF's own back cover: **Số 37 Lô TT01 Mon City, ngõ 4 Hàm
Nghi, Từ Liêm, Hà Nội** — matches the site's `mon-city` location. Prices are
printed VAT-exclusive.

> **This is NOT identical to the Golden Palace / Mễ Trì menu**
> (`golden-palace.md`), even though both come from the same overall MUGI menu
> book design. Ramen, Otsumami (grills), Agemono, Cơm (page 2), Udon mains,
> soft drinks, and the full Alcoholic Drinks section are byte-identical
> across both. But this branch's book **drops several items** (3 Sashimi,
> 2 Salad, Umaki, Trứng Hấp, Kem Mochi) and **adds/swaps a few others**
> (2 extra sushi rolls, drops 1 roll, a different grilled-squid dish, renamed
> rice bowls, an extra gelato flavor). Every difference below is called out
> inline — do not assume parity with Golden Palace for any item not marked
> identical.

Format: **Vietnamese name** *(English name)* — Japanese name — price
