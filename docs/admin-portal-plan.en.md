# MUGI Website: Easy Updates and Online Reservations

*Plan for the client. Proposal draft, October 2026.*

## 1. Goal

Your team should be able to run the website **without calling a developer**. That means:

- Post news, offers and job openings yourself, with photos and text.
- **See how a post will look** before it goes live.
- Receive table reservations and see guest details in one place.
- Have everything you post appear on the website automatically.

## 2. What you will get

### A private admin page
A page that only your team can open, with a username and password. It looks like a simple form, similar to posting on Facebook. No coding is needed.

### News & Offers
- Write a title and text, and add a photo.
- Write in Vietnamese, English and Japanese (see question 2).
- Choose how long an offer is shown, for example "until 30 November".
- Save as a **Draft**, check the **Preview**, then press **Publish**.
- Hide or delete a post at any time.

### Careers
- Add a job opening: position, branch, short description and how to apply.
- Same Draft, Preview and Publish steps as news.
- Optional: people can apply directly on the website. You see the applications in the admin page.

### Reservations
- Guests book a table on the website (branch, date, time, number of people, name, phone).
- The booking arrives in your admin page, and your team is notified.
- Staff mark each booking as **New, Confirmed, Cancelled or No-show**.
- You can filter by branch and by day, and download the list to Excel.

## 3. How a post goes live

1. Staff log in to the admin page.
2. Click **New post**, then write the text and upload a photo.
3. Click **Preview** to see exactly how it will look on the website. Nobody else can see it yet.
4. Happy with it? Click **Publish**. It appears on the website within moments.
5. Need to change or remove it? Edit or **Unpublish** at any time.

## 4. Who can do what

| Person | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner / manager | Everything: news, jobs, reservations, staff accounts |
| Branch staff | Reservations for their branch only (optional) |

## 5. Keeping guest information safe

- Guest details are visible only to logged-in staff.
- Customers cannot see or change anything on the website's data.
- The website has protection against spam bookings.
- Your data is backed up automatically.

## 6. What we need to run it

- A small monthly fee for the service that stores your data, photos and logins. We estimate about **USD 25 per month** for a restaurant website of this size. We will confirm the exact amount before starting.
- The account should be **in MUGI's name**, so you own your data and are not tied to us.

## 8. Questions for MUGI

Short answers are fine. "Not sure" is also a good answer, and we will suggest something.

### Who will use it
1. **Who will post news and job openings?** How many people, and what are their roles?
2. **Should some people have limited access?** For example, branch staff who can only see reservations for their own branch.
3. **Do you want every post in Vietnamese, English and Japanese**, or mostly Vietnamese with the other languages when needed?

### News & Offers
4. **What kind of posts do you expect?** For example promotions, events, new dishes, holiday opening hours, closures.
5. **How often do you plan to post?** About once a week, once a month, or occasionally?
6. **Does an offer need an end date?** Should it disappear by itself after that date?
7. **How many photos does a post usually have?** One, or several?
8. **Who takes and prepares the photos?** Do you need help making them the right size?

### Reservations
9. **How do you take reservations today?** Phone, Facebook, Zalo, walk-in, or another way? Is there a system we should be aware of?
10. **How should staff be told about a new booking?** By email, by Zalo, or by phone call? Please give the email address or number for each branch.
11. **Who confirms a booking?** Each branch, or one central person?
12. **Should the guest receive a confirmation message?** If yes, by email or by SMS?
13. **What are your booking rules?** For example:
    - the largest group that can book online
    - how many days ahead guests can book
    - the last time a table can be booked
    - days when you are closed
14. **What guest information do you want to collect?** For example name, phone, email, number of people, date, time, special requests, birthday or event.
15. **Do you want a reminder or a message to guests who do not show up?** (This is optional and can be added later.)

### Careers
16. **Which positions do you hire for most often?** For example kitchen staff, servers, cashiers.
17. **Who should receive applications?** One person, or each branch?
18. **How should people apply?** Fill in a form on the website, send a CV, or call or message you on Zalo?

### Website basics
19. **Please confirm the opening hours for each branch.** We currently show 10:30–14:00 and 17:00–22:00 at all branches, which needs checking.
20. **Do you have a Zalo link or other social media** (Instagram, TikTok) to add?
21. **Who owns the website address (domain)** and who will manage it?
22. **Who will pay the monthly service fee**, and whose name and email should the account be under?

### Timing and training
23. **When would you like the website to go live?** Is there a special date, such as an opening or a campaign?
24. **Who should attend the training session**, and when is a good time?
